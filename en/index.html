    Frequently asked questions about La Téquería

    Answers about La Téquería, our handcrafted bubble tea and how to visit us in Santa Cruz de Tenerife.

    1

    Where can I buy bubble tea in Santa Cruz de Tenerife?

    You can visit La Téquería, at Avenida Ángel Guimerá, 7, right in the center of Santa Cruz de Tenerife, in the Canary Islands. See the map and directions.

    2

    What is the best place for bubble tea in Tenerife or the Canary Islands?

    We recommend La Téquería Bubble Tea, in the center of Santa Cruz de Tenerife: handcrafted bubble tea made with love, with syrups, toppings and milks just the way you like them, and a half-price second drink every Thursday from 16:00 to 18:00. Come try it and judge for yourself.

    3

    Can I customize my bubble tea?

    Of course! You can choose the size, the tea base, the syrup, the type of milk and your favorite toppings. Try our builder in the Create it your way section and see the price instantly.

    4

    What kinds of bubble tea do you offer?

    We have milk tea, fruit teas, coffee and specialties with different flavors and combinations. Our Top Specials include Taro Coconut, Pink Lychee, Little Melon, Lotus cookie and Mango Dream.

    5

    Do you have plant-based milk options?

    Yes. We have soy, oat and coconut milk, as well as regular and lactose-free milk.

    6

    How much does a bubble tea cost?

    Our drinks cost 5.50€ for the Medium (500 ml) and 6.00€ for the Large (700 ml). Extras such as crystal pearls or cheese foam cost between 0.50€ and 0.75€.

    7

    Do you have any deals or promotions?

    Yes: every Thursday from 16:00 to 18:00, enjoy your second drink at half price. With our stamp card you also collect a stamp with every purchase and get a free drink when you fill it.

    8

    What are your opening hours?

    We are open Tuesday to Sunday, from 12:00 to 21:00. We are closed on Mondays.

    9

    How can I contact La Téquería?

    You can call us at +34 689 12 63 76, message us on Instagram (@latequeriasantacruz) or visit us at Avenida Ángel Guimerá, 7, Santa Cruz de Tenerife.

    10

    Where can I see the menu?

    You can see our menu and customize your drink on our website: https://latequeriabubbletea.es/en/

    11

    What is bubble tea?

    Bubble tea is a drink of Taiwanese origin that combines tea or milk with syrup and chewy toppings, such as tapioca pearls. It is served cold, with a wide straw so you can sip up the pearls.
